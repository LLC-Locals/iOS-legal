Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Locals ("Locals", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, shares, retains, and deletes information when you use the Locals iOS app and related services.

Summary

  • Locals uses account information to create and secure your account.
  • Locals uses location permission to show nearby places, center the map, and improve place search.
  • Locals accesses photos or videos only when you choose media for upload.
  • Locals uses third-party services including Firebase, Google Sign-In, Google Places, and Mapbox to provide app features.
  • Locals does not sell personal information and does not use collected data for third-party advertising unless this policy is updated and required consent is obtained.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly, information generated when you use app features, and information processed by service providers integrated into the app.

Account and profile information

  • Email address, name, surname, nickname, profile photo URL, and Firebase user identifier.
  • Authentication tokens used to keep you signed in and protect requests to our backend.
  • If you create an account with email and password, authentication is handled through Firebase Authentication. We do not store your raw password in the Locals app.
  • If you sign in with Google, Google may provide your name, email address, profile photo, and authentication tokens needed to sign you in.

Content and app activity

  • Guides, itineraries, favorite places, saved places, place lists, and other content you create or edit in Locals.
  • Place information such as place names, Google place identifiers, addresses, categories, coordinates, and photos associated with places.
  • Account settings and profile updates.

Location information

  • Approximate or precise device location, if you grant iOS location permission.
  • Location is used to center maps, show nearby places, and bias place search results.
  • You can disable location access at any time in iOS Settings.

Photos, videos, and media metadata

  • Photos or videos you select for upload, such as place photos.
  • Media metadata needed to process uploads, such as file type, file name, file extension, dimensions, and file size.
  • You can manage photo library permission in iOS Settings.

Device, diagnostics, and network information

  • Device and app diagnostics, crash reports, performance data, and basic technical logs may be processed to maintain security and reliability.
  • Network requests may include IP address, app version, device information, timestamps, and request metadata needed to provide the service and prevent abuse.

2. How We Use Information

Purpose Examples
Provide core app features Create accounts, sign users in, show maps, search places, save guides, save itineraries, manage favorites, and upload place photos.
Personalize app content Show nearby places, remember saved places, and display profile information.
Security and fraud prevention Authenticate requests, protect accounts, detect misuse, and keep the service available.
Support and communication Respond to support requests, account deletion requests, and privacy requests.
Diagnostics and improvement Investigate crashes, fix bugs, monitor performance, and improve reliability.

3. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

We share information only as needed to operate Locals, comply with law, protect users, or with your direction. Third-party service providers are expected to protect user data in a manner consistent with this policy and applicable law.

  • Firebase Authentication and Firebase services: used for account authentication, app configuration, and diagnostics.
  • Google Sign-In: used when you choose to authenticate with your Google account.
  • Google Places: used for place search, place details, place identifiers, addresses, coordinates, categories, ratings, and place photos.
  • Mapbox: used to display maps and support map/location features.
  • Backend and infrastructure providers: used to store accounts, content, media, tokens, and operational logs.

We may also disclose information if required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of Locals, users, or others.

4. Tracking, Advertising, and Sale of Data

Locals does not sell personal information. Based on the current app functionality, Locals does not use personal information to track users across other companies' apps or websites for third-party advertising. If these practices change, we will update this policy and request any consent required by iOS, App Store rules, and applicable law.

5. Your Choices and Controls

  • Location: grant, deny, or revoke location permission in iOS Settings.
  • Photos: grant, limit, or revoke photo library permission in iOS Settings.
  • Account: update account information in the app where available.
  • Google account access: manage or revoke Google account permissions from your Google account settings.
  • Sign out: sign out from the app to clear the active local session.

6. Account Deletion and Data Deletion

You can request account deletion from within the Locals app through the account settings deletion flow, where available. You can also contact us at [privacy@example.com].

When an account is deleted, we delete or de-identify account information and user content unless we need to keep limited information for legal, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, backup, or accounting reasons. Backup copies may persist for a limited period before being overwritten according to our backup lifecycle.

7. Data Retention

  • Account and profile data is retained while your account is active.
  • User content is retained until you delete it, delete your account, or request deletion, subject to the exceptions described above.
  • Authentication tokens are retained only as needed to keep you signed in and secure app requests.
  • Diagnostic logs are retained for the period needed to maintain security, reliability, and legal compliance.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. International Transfers

Locals and its service providers may process information in countries other than your country of residence. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

10. Children's Privacy

Locals is not intended for children under 13, or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to Locals, contact us so we can take appropriate action.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date and provide additional notice where required by law or platform rules.
